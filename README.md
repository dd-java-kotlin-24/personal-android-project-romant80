# P.A.U.L.A. (Plan, Act, Uplift: Life Achieved)



## Summary


For my primary project, I want to build a personal task manager and daily routine companion in memory of my late girlfriend, 
who loved keeping our living spaces neat, organized, and running smoothly. The app combines a clean daily checklist for
household chores and personal goals with an interactive 2D avatar and custom audio clips that provide voice encouragement 
throughout the day. You check off daily routines—like tidying rooms, folding blankets, or family to-dos—and the avatar
reacts with spoken affirmations when you complete tasks or maintain a daily streak. It is designed to be an encouraging,
grounded organizer that makes staying on top of daily life feel personal and uplifting.
